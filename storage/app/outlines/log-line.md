## Logline
One or two sentences that capture the core premise and tension.

## Story Beats

### 1. Opening
What we see first. Establish character, voice, tone, and situation.

### 2. Inciting Incident
The moment things change. What sets the plot in motion?

### 3. Rising Action
What complications or escalations follow?

### 4. Climax
The turning point. The action from the prompt lands here.

### 5. Resolution
How does it end? What has changed?
